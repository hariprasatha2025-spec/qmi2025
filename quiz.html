QMI 2026 Quiz Competition

Test Your Knowledge. Challenge Your Thinking.

The QMI 2026 Quiz Competition is an interactive knowledge-based activity designed to encourage active learning, participation, quick thinking, and engagement throughout the five-day workshop.

Participants will take part in quiz rounds covering themes related to Quantum Mathematics, Quantum Computing, Biomedical Imaging, Artificial Intelligence, Healthcare, Science, and emerging technologies.

🎯 Objectives

  • Encourage active learning and participation.
  • Promote quick thinking and problem-solving.
  • Reinforce concepts discussed during the workshop.
  • Identify participants who demonstrate strong knowledge and engagement.

🏆 Recognition & Awards

Outstanding participants will be recognized during the valedictory session with the following awards:

🏆 QMI Young Scholar Award
UG/PG Students
🏆 QMI Research Excellence Award
Research Scholars
🏆 QMI Distinguished Professional Award
Faculty Members and Industry Professionals

Awardees will receive a memento and certificate of recognition in appreciation of their active participation and academic excellence demonstrated during the workshop.
